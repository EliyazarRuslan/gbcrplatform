What's your primary goal with the mobile apps?

This shapes the entire architecture, scope, and timeline

A

Full Parity

Rebuild the entire GBCR platform as native mobile apps — fleet management, bookings, inspections, analytics, AI insights, customer management, and admin. Everything the web app does, but on mobile.

B

Field-Focused

Mobile apps for field staff — inspections (with camera/GPS), bookings, fleet status lookups. Admin, analytics, and heavy management stay on the web where they belong.

C

Customer-Facing

A separate app for rental customers — browse vehicles, make bookings, view their agreements, track active rentals. Distinct from the internal staff platform.

D

Hybrid (Staff + Customer)

Two apps: one for field staff (inspections, bookings, fleet) and one for customers (browse, book, track). Each focused on its audience.
