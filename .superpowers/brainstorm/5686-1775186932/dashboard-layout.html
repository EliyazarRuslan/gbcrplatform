B — Command Center List

Top nav instead of sidebar. KPI summary up top (live/deploying/failed counts). Projects in a compact list with inline deploy/retry buttons. More data-dense, ops-focused.

Recommendation: B — Command Center

For a deploy-focused v1 with 4-8 projects, the list view is more efficient. You see everything at a glance without clicking into cards. The KPI bar gives instant health status. Sidebar can come in v2 when you add editor/terminal features.
